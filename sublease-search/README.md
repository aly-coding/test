# Sublease Search

A one-page site about a project to find commercial space for sublease, built as a Design canvas page.

## Contents

- `project/Main.dc.html`: the page source (hero with a sample stacking plan, the requirements brief, why sublease, how the search runs, a list of San Francisco coffee shops that close by 5 pm, a shortlist table, pre-signing checks, and a contact block).
- `project/canvas.json`: the canvas index that places the page as one fluid artboard (1280 px wide).

## Coffee shop matches

The "San Francisco coffee shops that close by 5 pm" section lists eight shops whose Google listing showed a daily closing time of 5 pm or earlier on Oct 15, 2026. Hours can change and none of the listings say a shop wants to rent out its space, so each one needs a call to the owner.

## Placeholders

Values in square brackets, such as `[CITY]`, `[SQ FT]` and `[EMAIL ADDRESS]`, are placeholders to replace with the real project details.
